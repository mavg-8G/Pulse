Privacy Policy

Pulse (https://pulse.espectro.uk) · Effective October 14, 2026 · Version 1.0

Summary. Pulse is a private, invitation-only personal expense tracker available at pulse.espectro.uk. It processes only what it needs to work: your account, the expenses you record and, only if you choose to connect Gmail, the purchase notifications your bank sends to your inbox. Pulse does not sell data, shows no ads, has no analytics and does not use your data to train AI models. Data is stored on a server in Germany (European Union).

This policy applies to the Pulse web application and to the OAuth client named “Pulse” that requests access to Google accounts. It follows Ecuador’s Organic Law on Personal Data Protection (LOPDP) and, for users in the European Union or European Economic Area, the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR). Where both apply, the more protective rule is followed. The data controller is the administrator of this Pulse server, who invited you; contact them through the same channel you received your invitation for any privacy question or request.

1. Google user data (Gmail)

Connecting Gmail is optional and only happens when you press “Connect Gmail” in Settings and approve Google’s consent screen.

Data accessed

  • Scope requested: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly (read-only). Pulse does not request permission to send, modify or delete email.
  • Gmail address of the connected account (from the Gmail profile endpoint), to show you which account is connected.
  • OAuth refresh token issued by Google, to keep importing without asking you to sign in every time.
  • Only bank notification emails. Pulse searches Gmail with a query filtered on Google’s servers by sender (Banco Pichincha, Produbanco and Diners Club / Titanium notification addresses) and by date, for example from:(bank senders) newer_than:60d. Pulse never lists, opens or downloads any other email. For each matching message it reads the message content (subject, sender, date and body).

How the data is used

  • Solely to provide the user-facing feature you enabled: extract the amount, date, merchant name and last digits of the card from each bank purchase notification and create the corresponding expense in your Pulse account, marked “To review” until you confirm it.
  • The Gmail address is shown in Settings; the refresh token is used only to call the Gmail API on your behalf.
  • Optionally, if you separately enable “Gemini” in Settings (off by default, requires re-confirming your identity), a bank notification whose format Pulse does not recognize is sent to Google’s Gemini API to extract the same fields. Before sending, your name, email addresses and any sequence of 6 or more digits are removed.
  • Google user data is not used for advertising, is not sold, is not used to build user profiles, and is not used to develop, improve or train any artificial intelligence or machine learning model, whether Pulse’s or a third party’s.
  • No person reads your email. Humans may access the data derived from it only with your explicit consent (for example, to fix an import problem you report), when needed for security purposes such as investigating abuse, or to comply with the law.

How the data is stored and protected

  • Emails are never stored. Each notification is processed in memory and discarded immediately after the expense is extracted.
  • What is stored: the resulting expense (amount, date, account, category, and the merchant name encrypted), the Gmail address and refresh token encrypted with a key unique to your account (AES-256-GCM), and a keyed hash (HMAC) of each processed message ID so the same email is never imported twice.
  • All traffic between your browser, Pulse and Google uses HTTPS (TLS).

How the data is shared

  • Google user data is not shared, transferred or disclosed to any third party, except: (a) Google itself, through the Gmail API calls needed to read the notifications and, only if you enable it, the Gemini API as described above; (b) the hosting provider that runs the server (Contabo GmbH, Germany), which stores the encrypted data and has no access to its contents; (c) if required by law.

Retention and deletion

  • Message-ID hashes are deleted automatically after 60 days.
  • The Gmail address and refresh token are kept only while Gmail is connected.
  • Disconnect at any time in Settings → Bank email (Gmail) → Disconnect: Pulse revokes the token with Google, stops notifications and deletes the stored connection immediately. You can also revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Expenses already imported remain in your account until you delete them or delete your account.
  • You can ask the administrator to delete your account and all associated data at any time (see section 7).

Limited Use disclosure

Pulse’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Security

  • Each user has their own encryption key (AES-256-GCM), itself protected by a master key kept outside the database. It encrypts merchant, description and notes of each expense, merchant rules, the Gmail connection, the two-factor secret and push subscriptions.
  • HTTPS only, mandatory two-factor sign-in, expiring sessions and protected session cookies.
  • Invitation-only registration, tamper-resistant security log and alerts on important account changes.
  • Automatic backups on the same server before each update (the last 15 are kept).

If a breach affects your data, it will be notified to the supervisory authority and, where required, to you, within the deadlines of the LOPDP (art. 43) and the GDPR (arts. 33–34; 72 hours to the authority).

7. Your rights

You may at any time, free of charge, exercise the rights granted by the LOPDP (arts. 12–21) and the GDPR (arts. 15–22): access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction, portability (you receive your data as a structured JSON file), withdrawal of consent (Gmail, Gemini, notifications — directly in Settings) and not being subject to automated decisions with legal effects (Pulse only suggests categories; it makes no such decisions and builds no profiles).

To exercise them, contact the administrator of this Pulse server who invited you, from your account’s email address. You may be asked to confirm your identity. Requests are answered within 15 days. You may also lodge a complaint with Ecuador’s Superintendencia de Protección de Datos Personales or, if you live in the EU/EEA, with your national data protection authority.

8. Cookies and local storage

Pulse uses a single strictly necessary cookie, __Host-pulse, to keep you signed in (Secure, HttpOnly, SameSite=Strict). Your browser also stores your theme and animation preferences, and the app shell is cached for offline use (never your financial data). There are no third-party cookies, ads or analytics.

9. Children

Pulse is not intended for anyone under 18 and is available by personal invitation only.

10. Changes to this policy

Material changes will be announced in the app before they take effect, and the date above will be updated. If a change affects how Pulse uses Google user data, you will be notified and asked for consent again where required.
