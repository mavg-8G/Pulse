Pulse — your personal expense tracker

Pulse helps you see where your money goes. Record your expenses, organize them by account and category, set a monthly budget for each category and get notified before you overspend. Optionally, Pulse can read the purchase notifications your bank sends to your Gmail and turn them into expenses for you to review.

Pulse is a private, invitation-only web app (PWA) for personal use. It is free, has no ads and no analytics.

What Pulse does

Accounts and categories

Bank accounts, credit and debit cards, cash and wallets, with your own categories and subcategories.

Monthly plan

A budget per category, progress for the month and alerts at the thresholds you choose.

Manual entry

Add an expense in seconds from your phone or computer, with dates, notes and refunds.

Notifications

Push notifications for budgets, new expenses to review and security events on your account.

Works everywhere

Installable on mobile and desktop, light and dark mode, usable offline for the app shell.

Secure by default

Mandatory two-factor sign-in (passkeys or authenticator app) and per-user encryption of sensitive data.

Why Pulse asks for access to Gmail

Connecting Gmail is optional. If you connect it, Pulse requests the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly scope for one purpose only: to read the purchase notification emails sent by your bank (currently Banco Pichincha, Produbanco and Diners Club / Titanium) and create the corresponding expense in your account, so you don’t have to type it.

  • The Gmail search is filtered on Google’s side by the bank sender addresses and by date. Pulse never lists, opens or downloads any other email.
  • Each notification is processed in memory to extract the amount, date, merchant and last digits of the card, and is then discarded. Pulse does not store emails.
  • Imported expenses are marked “To review” until you confirm them.
  • Pulse never sends email, never modifies or deletes your email, and does not use Gmail data for advertising or to train AI models.
  • You can disconnect at any time from Settings; Pulse then revokes its access token with Google and deletes the connection.

Full details, including what is stored, for how long and who it is shared with, are in the Privacy Policy.

Who can use Pulse

Pulse is invitation-only: you need an invitation code from the person who administers this Pulse server. If you were invited, sign in or create your account.
